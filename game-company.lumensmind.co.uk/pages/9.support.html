Frequently asked questions

Concise answers about accounts, data, and decision workflows.

  • How often is the data updated?

    Connected sources are updated through daily synchronization. CSV uploads appear in Explore and decision dashboards immediately after commit.

  • Which actions are restricted in the demo studio?

    The demo account is read-only. Connector changes, ticket creation, and billing updates are disabled, but you can explore dashboards and decision lists.

  • How are decision recommendations generated?

    Baseline rules v0.x generates evidence-based recommendations from synchronized metrics and studio targets. Recommendations enter the review queue and are never applied automatically.

  • What should I do if a CSV import fails?

    Download the rejected rows from the validation step, verify the template headers, and upload the file again. If the issue persists, open a new support ticket.

  • Can I define separate targets for multiple games?

    Yes. ROAS, CPI, and budget thresholds can be configured independently for each game. Decision rules run in the context of the selected game.
