The decision layer for mobile UA

Lumensmind is not another dashboard. It turns noisy performance data into decision outputs your team can review, approve, and track — with evidence attached.

01

Command Center

One screen for what needs a decision today.

See prioritised decisions, live signals, and a compact KPI row — built for daily stand-ups, not endless tab switching.

02

Decisions

Queue, evidence, approve or reject.

Work through a ranked decision queue with lifecycle status, linked metrics, and human approval before anything changes in spend.

03

Scenario Lab

Test budget moves before you commit.

Run what-if simulations on budget allocation across networks and campaigns, then compare outcomes side by side.

04

Explore

Analytics when you need the drill-down.

Overview, Networks, Campaigns, Creatives, and Countries — structured tabs that support decisions instead of replacing them.

05

Decision Memory

A ledger of what you chose and what happened.

Track outcomes after you act so finance and growth can see the story behind each budget call over time.

Not another dashboard

Dashboards answer “what happened?” Lumensmind answers “what should we do next — and why?” Charts support the story; the decision queue is the product.
