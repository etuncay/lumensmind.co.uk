Our story

Lumensmind started with a simple observation: mobile UA teams drown in dashboards but still struggle to decide where the next pound of budget should go.

Founding

We founded Lumensmind to turn fragmented network data into prioritised, reviewable decisions — with humans always in the loop and connectors kept read-only.

Vision

Decision intelligence for mobile game UA: helping studios make better budget decisions with evidence, scenario comparison, and a clear audit trail of what was chosen and why.

Growth

Today we partner with early studios in the UK and Europe, expanding integrations and workflow depth while keeping transparency and data privacy at the centre of the product.
