How it works

From connection to prioritised queue in three steps.

  1. 1

    Connect

    Add read-only credentials or upload a CSV. Lumensmind never writes to your ad accounts.

  2. 2

    Sync

    Scheduled syncs land spend, installs, and revenue in your tenant-scoped workspace.

  3. 3

    Decisions

    The Baseline Engine turns unified metrics into prioritised decisions with evidence.

Data security

Connectors are read-only— Lumensmind pulls metrics but never changes campaigns in your networks. Credentials and tokens are encrypted at rest; each studio's data stays isolated by organisation.
