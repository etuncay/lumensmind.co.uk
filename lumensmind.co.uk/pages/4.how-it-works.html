From data to decisions in four steps

A transparent, rule-based path from your connectors to prioritised budget calls — with evidence, human approval, and a record of what happened next.

Step 1

Connect your data

Link Adjust, ad networks, and CSV imports through read-only connectors. Spend, installs, and revenue land in one tenant-scoped workspace — no write access to your ad accounts.

AdjustNetworksCSV
Lumensmind

Step 2

Baseline Engine analyses performance signals

The Baseline Engine ingests campaign and creative metrics, compares them to your rules and thresholds, and surfaces the performance signals that matter for budget decisions.

Step 3

Prioritised decisions with evidence and expected impact

Each recommendation arrives as a decision card: what to change, the evidence behind it, and the expected impact on spend or efficiency. Your queue is ranked so the highest-leverage calls rise to the top.

Step 4

You approve, implement, and track outcomes

Approve or reject in the workflow, implement changes in your networks, then record outcomes. Lumensmind stays in the loop — it recommends; your team decides what actually ships.

Decision Memory closes the loop

Outcomes from past calls feed the next cycle of recommendations. What you approved, what changed in spend, and what happened afterwards stay linked so future decisions inherit real studio history — not guesswork.

Results flow back into the Baseline Engine and your next prioritised queue.

Transparent, rule-based recommendations — not a black box

Every suggestion traces to metrics you can inspect and rules you can adjust. Lumensmind shows its working: signals, thresholds, and expected impact — so finance and growth review the same facts before anything goes live.
